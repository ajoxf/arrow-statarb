Armed entries. In re-entry mode a stretch to ±entry z (held for the confirmation samples) ARMS; the entry comes when z falls back to entry − re-entry back, inside the window. A stretch that falls through the window floor without an entry never came back: it has no money and counts in no win/loss tally. Arming is not carried across a restart, live or here.
Limits. The rows come from a paper copy of the algo itself on the same signal, so every rule, gate and exit is the algo's own. It fills at the decision spread the algo saw (the executable side when there is a book); slippage beyond the algo's own cost estimate is not modelled. Its daily-loss and loss-streak gates read its own results, not the real trades. An open paper position survives a restart (); an arming does not. A row without a known lot multiplier has no money and counts in no tally.
